Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 4, 2026

Plates to Purpose (“we,” “us,” or “our”) is committed to protecting the privacy of our users, including school administrators, cafeteria staff, student volunteers, and food donors. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information through our website and mobile application (the “App”).

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you register, we collect your name, email address, school or organization affiliation, and role.
  • Operational Data: For school cafeteria management, we process student attendance feeds (provided by the school) and food inventory data to calculate precise quantities and track waste.
  • Donation and Volunteer Data: We collect location data and contact information to coordinate food rescues between donors and volunteers.
  • Usage Data: We automatically collect information about how you interact with the App, such as device type, IP address, and pages visited.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To Provide Services: We use data to sync attendance with kitchen planning, calculate ingredient needs, and generate waste analytics.
  • To Coordinate Food Rescues: We use contact and location data to connect donors with volunteer drivers and receiving organizations.
  • To Improve the App: We use aggregated, de-identified usage data and AI to optimize menu planning and identify waste trends.
  • Compliance: To fulfill legal obligations and ensure the safety of our food rescue network.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

  • Service Providers: We may share data with third-party vendors who assist with hosting, analytics, and app functionality.
  • School Districts: Operational data related to food waste is shared with the respective school administration for reporting and management.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of our users.
  • No Sale of Data: We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Security

We implement administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your data. However, no system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of your information.

5. Children’s Privacy

Plates to Purpose is designed for use by school staff, adult volunteers, and students under school supervision. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 without verifiable parental or school consent.

6. Your Choices

You may access, update, or delete your account information through the App settings. For inquiries regarding data processed on behalf of a school, please contact your school administrator.
